## Physical test run order for 0.5.48 build 54

Run this on the HONOR with the EMEET S600 and the client Redmi with its deployment camera, hub, cable, charger, and site Wi-Fi. All steps are pending on physical devices for this candidate.

1. [ ] **Install and enroll.** Use the build 54 debug-signed APK on dedicated test tablets with a compatible signing certificate. Test an in-place upgrade with a disposable saved clip already queued. 2. [ ] **Check camera and player entry.** Confirm the three-second clip and mic source. Change camera mode, reconnect USB, and verify that player entry waits for a fresh camera check. In portrait and landscape, enter and edit four addresses with the actual docked keyboard. Verify that the field, Add email, and Review emails remain visible and the background stays fixed.
3. [ ] **Run full matches.** Record 15, 20, and 25 minutes at the deployment camera mode. Check logo, orientation, measured frame cadence, and sound through the end. Repeat with a screen lock and with an older video transferring. Time camera preparation and stop-to-next-match readiness.
4. [ ] **Exercise the queue.** Start a new match during validation, legacy logo processing where applicable, and Drive upload. Compare startup with 0, 1, 5, and 10 queued videos. Check that waiting and retry cooldown are distinct, retry keeps the right video, and an active transfer keeps its turn.
5. [ ] **Interrupt capture and recover.** Use disposable clips for USB disconnect, app switching, restart, screen lock, system/audio interruptions, and storage or power pressure. Confirm recoverable footage is preserved, can be exported, and does not block the next match after recovery.
6. [ ] **Verify staging delivery and cleanup.** Interrupt Wi-Fi during a real Drive upload and resume. Compare APK, Admin, and recording logs using the same session ID. Verify the downloaded replay, recipient email, interrupted-clip message when applicable, local-copy cleanup after confirmation, and playback of the separately exported copy.
7. [ ] **Run the overnight soak.** Use the Redmi in its intended kiosk configuration with back-to-back matches and intermittent connectivity. Record storage headroom, thermal behavior, queue drain time, and the final outcome of every test clip.
